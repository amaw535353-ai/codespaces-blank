# Ethics and Safety

## Principles

- Obtain authorization before active testing.
- Respect privacy, ownership, consent, and legal boundaries.
- Minimize impact and collect only necessary evidence.
- Prefer reversible actions.
- Keep humans responsible for consequential decisions.
- Report uncertainty and stop when authorization is unclear.
- Never claim compliance, impact, or success without evidence.

## Human control

Ahmed decides whether testing starts, continues, stops, or expands.

AI assistance can draft tests, explain commands, inspect evidence, and propose fixes.

AI assistance cannot grant authorization or approve public disclosure.

Review every generated command before execution. Confirm its target, effect, limits, and recovery method.

## Privacy and dignity

Do not use real people in attack scenarios. Do not infer or expose private personal information.

Use invented identities and harmless content. Mark synthetic artifacts clearly.

## Harm prevention

Do not degrade services, destroy data, bypass billing, evade monitoring, or create persistence.

Do not reuse findings against other systems. Do not share exploit details before remediation.

## Safety hierarchy

1. Protect people and their data.
2. Stay within written authorization.
3. Protect systems and service availability.
4. Preserve reliable evidence.
5. Achieve the learning objective.

If goals conflict, follow the higher item.

## Cost safety

Use no paid APIs, billable cloud resources, paid SaaS, or credit-card trials.

Stop before any action that may create an unapproved charge.

## Research integrity

- Separate observations from interpretations.
- Record failed and successful tests.
- Do not exaggerate severity.
- Verify scanner output manually.
- Document limitations and alternative explanations.
- Correct public material when new evidence changes a conclusion.
